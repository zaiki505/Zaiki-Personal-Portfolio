CarPawl: Offline-First Carpool Fuel Tracker

Tracking fuel fill-ups and fairly splitting carpool costs between passengers, running fully on-device with no backend and no account required.

Project Overview

CarPawl solves a simple problem: figuring out who owes what after a shared car ride. Log fuel fill-ups by cost, litres, or distance, then split each trip by distance, equally, or with custom rules. It keeps a running balance so you always know who owes who. Everything is stored locally with Dexie (IndexedDB), so it works offline and keeps no personal data on a server. Available as an installable PWA and a native Android app via Capacitor. Optional Google Drive sync backs up your data and keeps devices in sync, with proper handling for deletions and conflicts.

Key Features

Flexible Fuel Logging Record fill-ups by cost, litres, or distance travelled
Three Split Modes Distance-weighted, equal, or a custom pool with maintenance markup
Recurring Trips & Prepay Daily to yearly trips auto-schedule, with advance payments held until the date arrives
Google Drive Sync Keeps your data backed up and synced across devices, with safe conflict handling.
Offline-First Storage Dexie and IndexedDB, fully functional with no network
Native Android Build Capacitor 8 shell with haptics, biometric lock, and local notifications
