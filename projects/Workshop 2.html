Short Film & Animation Project

Workshop 2: Inside the Frame

A hybrid short film about a man stuck in a daily loop, told through light, colour and animation instead of dialogue.

Project Overview

Inside the Frame is a 5 to 7 minute short film about Adam, an office worker whose days follow the same routine. He is not a bad person, but years of isolation have slowly changed how he treats the people around him. The film follows that change in small moments and asks what it takes to notice it. It was made for Workshop II under the Rukun Negara theme. The sub-theme, Kepercayaan kepada Tuhan, is expressed through conscience and moral responsibility. The film mixes live action with animation, 2D motion graphics, 3D elements and green screen compositing.
